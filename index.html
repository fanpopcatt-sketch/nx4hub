FAQ

Questions we get daily

How fast is delivery?

Instant. Once payment is confirmed your key is sent straight to you in the Discord ticket — 24/7, no manual approval queue.

How do I pay?

Open a ticket and tell us which product you want. We accept CashApp, PayPal, Crypto (including Phantom wallet), Venmo, Robux, and Minecraft accounts as trade.

What’s the difference between Minecraft Mod, Injection and EXE?

Mod ($3) is the standard mod menu and client package. Injection ($10) is the ghost client that injects into your instance. EXE ($8) is the standalone executable build for people who don’t want a client install.

Is RobRun the same as Roblox?

No — they’re two separate products. RobRun ($4) is its own dedicated suite for the RobRun game, and Roblox ($3) is the script hub and executor package for Roblox experiences. Buying one does not unlock the other.

Are updates included?

Yes. Every key includes all future updates for the game it was bought for. No renewals, no subscription.

Do I need a second PC?

No. Everything runs on the same Windows PC. A normal mouse and keyboard is enough.

Can I get banned?

Any third-party tool carries risk — every game’s terms forbid them, and publishers can ban accounts. We keep builds external and updated, but no one can promise zero detection. Use at your own risk.

How do I get support?

Join the NX4 Hub Discord and open a ticket. Staff walk you through install, config and troubleshooting.
